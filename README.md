Keep Calm and Clear
​Keep Calm and Clear is a lighthearted productivity and mindfulness project designed to help you declutter your mind, manage stress, and focus on one task at a time.

​📌 Project Overview
​In today's fast-paced world, constant notifications and multitasking can lead to information overload. This project provides simple techniques, quick mental check-ins, and actionable routines to clear your head and stay composed under pressure.

​✨ Features
​Mental Reset Routines: Quick 2-minute breathing and grounding exercises.
​Task Simplification: Strategies to break down overwhelming goals into clear, manageable steps.
​Distraction Blocker Tips: Simple habits to reduce digital noise and stay focused.
​Daily Reflection Prompts: Short end-of-day questions to clear mental backlog.

​🛠️ How to Use
​Pause: Take a deep breath whenever you feel overwhelmed.
​Prioritize: Write down the single most important task for the next hour.
​Execute: Put away unnecessary browser tabs and focus on one thing.
​Reflect: Spend 5 minutes reviewing what went well at the end of the day.

​💡 Quick Tips for Daily Clarity
​The 5-Minute Rule: If a task takes less than 5 minutes, do it immediately to prevent mental clutter.
​Single-Tasking: Close unused apps and focus exclusively on the primary objective.
​Brain Dump: When your mind feels full, write everything down on paper to clear your mental space.
